# Contributing

## Repository conventions

This repository is intended to act as a structured product and research workspace. Contributions should be organized in a way that keeps the thinking process traceable and reusable.

## Recommended practices

- Keep files in the correct section based on the product lifecycle stage.
- Use descriptive, consistent names with clear purpose.
- Add context to drafts and research notes so that others can understand the rationale.
- Separate raw research from decisions or finalized documentation.
- Keep privacy, safety, and ethical content boundaries in review documents rather than as an afterthought.

## Template use

Before creating new product or research artifacts, check the files in `templates/` for format guidance.

## File naming examples

- `User_Personas.md`
- `Competitor_Analysis.md`
- `Product_Requirements.md`
- `Release_Notes.md`

## Documentation quality

A high-quality update should:

- be specific and actionable
- describe assumptions clearly
- cite evidence or context when relevant
- avoid unsupported claims
- align with the product objective and user needs
