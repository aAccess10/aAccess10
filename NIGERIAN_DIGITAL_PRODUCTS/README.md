# Nigerian Digital Products Repository

This repository is organized as a working template for product research, documentation, content planning, design, product development, marketing, and testing for a Nigerian-focused digital product portfolio.

## Purpose

The structure below supports the full lifecycle of a digital product initiative:

- product framing and strategy
- market research and customer insight
- evidence collection and source material management
- product requirements and UX documentation
- content creation planning
- privacy and safety review
- design and prototype development
- application buildout
- go-to-market planning
- validation and improvement

## Working Standards

- Use clear, descriptive filenames.
- Keep evidence, assumptions, and source material separate from final product decisions.
- Document decisions with dates and owners where possible.
- Treat privacy, safety, and content boundaries as mandatory product requirements.
- Use the templates in `templates/` as a baseline for new product work.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Define the vision and strategy.
2. Research the Nigerian market and user segments.
3. Capture source material and evidence.
4. Produce product requirements and UX documentation.
5. Build content and design assets.
6. Validate with privacy and safety review.
7. Develop and test product applications.
8. Launch with marketing assets and measurement.
9. Review feedback and iterate.
